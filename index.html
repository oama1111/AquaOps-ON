AquaOps ON

A compliance-scheduling and water-quality early-warning platform that converts Ontario regulation O. Reg. 170/03 sampling obligations and chlorine-trend data into an executable weekly work plan for one- and two-person drinking water operations.

System at a glance

Small Ontario water systems are run by one or two certified operators who carry personal liability for missed sampling and late adverse-result reporting. AquaOps ON encodes the regulation as versioned, section-traceable data (YAML rules verified against O. Reg. 170/03), detects chlorine decay with an explainable EWMA + Isolation Forest detector, and schedules duties around regulatory windows with a greedy + 2-opt route planner. Every compliance record lives in an append-only audit trail.

AquaOps ON is a decision-support prototype. Its output is not legal proof of compliance; licensed-operator judgment prevails. Regulation data paraphrases O. Reg. 170/03 under the Safe Drinking Water Act, 2002 (King's Printer for Ontario). Datasets used under the Open Government Licence – Ontario.
